# My Hero Academia

## About My Hero Academia
**My Hero Academia** is a popular superhero anime and manga series set in a world where most people have superpowers called "Quirks."

There are two main groups:

- **Pro Heroes** - The protectors who use their Quirks to save people and stop crime.
- **Villains** - Those who use their Quirks for evil and seek to destroy hero society.
